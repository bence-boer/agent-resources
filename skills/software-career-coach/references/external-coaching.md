# External Coaching

Read this file when the user is deciding whether to hire a career coach, comparing coaching to mentoring, or trying to fix repeated non-technical career blockers.

## Boundary Definitions

| Support type | Main mechanism | Best fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mentor | shares personal pattern library and advice | navigating a path someone else has already walked |
| Coach | uses structured questions and reflection to unlock the user's own decisions | blind spots, transitions, leadership growth, repeated stalls |
| Consultant | diagnoses and prescribes directly | specialized project or process problems |
| Therapist | addresses mental health, trauma, or deeper emotional distress | burnout, anxiety, depression, or non-career distress |

## When External Coaching Helps

Recommend external coaching when at least 4 of these 6 conditions are true:

- the problem is repeated behavioral execution under pressure, not mainly missing information
- the user repeatedly misses promotions despite strong technical performance
- the user struggles with influence, conflict, executive communication, or negotiation
- the user is moving from IC to leadership and keeps solving people problems like code problems
- internal manager or mentor support has already been tried, or cannot be used safely because of confidentiality or politics
- success can be defined in work outcomes such as a promotion packet, executive communication, conflict handling, or leadership transition

The strongest signals are repeated behavioral execution under pressure and lack of safe internal support, but they do not lower the threshold.

Do NOT recommend external coaching yet when the need is mostly information, interview tactics, salary data, team diagnostics, mental-health treatment, or legal or financial advice.

## Coach Evaluation Criteria

- real software or engineering-leadership context, not generic business coaching only
- clear methodology rather than vague inspiration
- evidence of success with software-career transitions such as senior to Staff or principal scope expansion, IC to EM transition, executive communication, repeated promotion stalls, or conflict and negotiation inside engineering orgs
- good boundary hygiene around therapy, legal, and financial topics
- a structure for assessment, practice, feedback, and follow-through

## Typical Engagement Arc

1. assess goals, strengths, blind spots, and current organizational context
2. define measurable objectives
3. practice hard conversations and decision framing
4. apply the work to live situations
5. install habits that sustain change without the coach

## Coaching Moves

- If the user mainly needs information, do not push coaching.
- If the user needs repeated behavioral change under pressure, coaching is more plausible.
- If the threshold above is not met, do not recommend external coaching yet.
- If the issue is burnout or mental health, point outside coaching and avoid pretending career strategy alone solves it.
