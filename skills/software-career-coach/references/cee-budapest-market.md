# CEE and Budapest Market Calibration

Read this file only when the user explicitly needs Central or Eastern European market context, Budapest salary calibration, or region-specific networking signals.

## Market Shape

- Budapest is a strong regional tech hub with both multinational engineering centers and fast-moving startups.
- Scarcity remains especially strong in cloud, DevOps, AI, machine learning, and data engineering.
- Use the salary table below only for Budapest or Hungary-employed gross monthly roles.
- For broader CEE questions, give directional commentary only and ask for the specific country before quantifying.
- EU pay-transparency rules arriving in 2026 may improve range visibility, but current offers still need local calibration.
- Local company bands and employment models can differ sharply from US-centric advice.

## Local Decision Rules

- **If** the role is a Budapest local FTE, **then** use the gross monthly table as the first compensation anchor.
- **If** the employer is a multinational engineering center, **then** expect upper-half pressure for scarce skills, leadership scope, or internationally benchmarked teams.
- **If** the role is a foreign remote employer or a EUR or USD B2B contract, **then** do not map it 1:1 to Budapest gross monthly bands.
- **If** the question turns on contractor tax optimization, legal structure, or regulatory interpretation, **then** stop at market-strategy guidance and recommend local specialist advice.
