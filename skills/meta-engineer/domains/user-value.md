# Domain - User value

## Use when

The change affects what people can do, feel, trust, or notice.

## Route next

- [workflow-fit.md](workflow-fit.md) to place that value inside the surrounding journey
- [error-handling.md](error-handling.md) to preserve trust when the ideal path breaks
- [../finish/self-review.md](../finish/self-review.md) to ask whether the result is actually worth shipping

## Apply

- Solve the real user or operator problem, not just the wording of the task.
- Optimize for clarity, trust, and reduced friction.
- Use defaults, feedback, and guardrails to make the right action easy.
- Avoid adding ceremony unless it clearly removes greater risk or confusion.
- If the behavior changes, make the gain visible.

## Resolve explicitly

- What outcome actually improves?
- What would still feel broken even if the task is technically complete?
- What tradeoff are you making on the user's behalf?
- Are you fixing the cause or hiding the symptom?

## Minimum proof

- Intended user or operator flow demonstrates the improvement
- Behavior change is understandable from the surface
- Risk and convenience tradeoffs are not accidental
